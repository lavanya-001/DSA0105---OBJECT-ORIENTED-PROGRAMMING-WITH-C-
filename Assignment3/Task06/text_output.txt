RULES OF OPERATOR OVERLOADING
1. At least one operand must be a user-defined type.
2. Operator precedence cannot be changed.
3. Number of operands cannot be changed.
4. Operators cannot be created or invented.
5. Some operators cannot be overloaded.

Non-overloadable operators: .  .*  ::  ?:  sizeof
